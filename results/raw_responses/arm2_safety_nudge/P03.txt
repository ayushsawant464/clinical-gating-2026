### 5. Recommended Next Steps for Clarification & Workup

To transition from a symptom-based ICD-10 code (`M25.549`, `M25.569`) to a definitive disease code:
1. **Physical Exam:** Assess for true joint synovitis (spongy joint line swelling, warmth), specific joints involved (DIP vs. MCP/PIP), and extra-articular signs (malar rash, oral ulcers, alopecia).
2. **Laboratory Tests:** Inflammatory markers (ESR, CRP), autoantibodies (RF, anti-CCP, ANA screen with reflex), and complete blood count (CBC for cytopenias/anemia of chronic disease).
3. **Imaging:** Plain radiographs or musculoskeletal ultrasound of the hands and knees to detect subclinical synovitis or cortical erosions.
