### Clinical Assessment & ICD-10 Classification

#### 1. Explicit Assumptions & Clarifications
* **Clinical Underspecification:** The prompt provides only isolated, subjective constitutional symptoms without objective physical examination findings (e.g., lymphadenopathy, organomegaly), laboratory data (e.g., CBC, LDH, ESR/CRP, HIV, QuantiFERON), or diagnostic imaging.
* **ICD-10-CM Coding Assumption (Section I.B.4):** Official coding guidelines dictate that in outpatient/ambulatory evaluations where a definitive disease has **not** yet been established, the primary code must represent the documented signs and symptoms rather than speculative or unconfirmed diagnoses.

### Primary Clinical Diagnosis

* **Provisional / Symptom Diagnosis:** **Unexplained / Abnormal Weight Loss with Drenching Night Sweats (Systemic Constitutional / "B" Symptoms)**
* **Top Working Differential Diagnoses (Requires Immediate Workup):**
  1. **Hematologic Malignancy:** Lymphoma (Hodgkin lymphoma, Non-Hodgkin lymphoma)
  2. **Infection:** Tuberculosis (pulmonary/extrapulmonary), HIV infection, occult abscess, subacute bacterial endocarditis
  3. **Solid Tumor Malignancy:** Occult carcinoma (e.g., gastrointestinal, renal cell, lung)
  4. **Endocrine / Systemic:** Hyperthyroidism, pheochromocytoma, autoimmune/vasculitic disorders

### Recommended Next Diagnostic Steps
1. **Physical Exam:** Complete nodal survey (cervical, supraclavicular, axillary, inguinal), hepatosplenomegaly check, skin/chest exam.
2. **Laboratory Workup:** CBC with peripheral blood smear, CMP, ESR, CRP, LDH, TSH, HIV 1/2 antigen/antibody, and QuantiFERON-TB Gold.
3. **Imaging:** Contrast-enhanced CT scan (neck, chest, abdomen, pelvis) or chest radiograph (CXR).
4. **Tissue Biopsy:** Excisional lymph node biopsy if lymphadenopathy is present.
