### Primary Diagnosis & Coding

* **Primary Clinical Diagnosis:** **Lumbago with Sciatica (Right-sided Lumbar Radiculopathy)**
* **Primary ICD-10-CM Code:** **`M54.41`** (*Lumbago with sciatica, right side*)

---

### Explicit Assumptions & Clinical Rationale

1. **Coding Basis (Symptom vs. Structural Etiology):**
   * The patient presents with concurrent lower back pain ("aching constantly") and radiating radicular pain down the ipsilateral extremity ("shoots down my right leg"). In ICD-10-CM, when low back pain and sciatica are reported together, combination code **`M54.4-`** is indicated.
   * Laterality is specifically right-sided (`M54.41`).

2. **Absence of Confirmatory Diagnostic Data:**
   * In the absence of neuroimaging (MRI/CT of the lumbar spine) or electrodiagnostic studies (EMG/NCS), structural etiologies—such as a herniated nucleus pulposus (**`M51.16`**) or lumbar spinal stenosis (**`M48.061`**) with neurogenic claudication (which is common in this age group and often worsens with prolonged standing)—cannot be confirmed. Coding is therefore assigned based on the primary clinical presentation.

---

### Key Differential Diagnoses (Pending Imaging / Physical Exam)

* **`M48.061`** – Spinal stenosis, lumbar region, with neurogenic claudication
* **`M51.16`** – Intervertebral disc disorders with radiculopathy, lumbar region
* **`M43.16`** – Spondylolisthesis, lumbar region
