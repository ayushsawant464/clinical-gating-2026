* **Primary Clinical Diagnosis:** **Irritable Bowel Syndrome with mixed bowel habits (IBS-M)** *(Provisional)*

---

### Explicit Assumptions & Coding Nuances

1. **Working Diagnosis vs. Symptom-Based Coding:**
   * **Clinical Working Diagnosis (`K58.2` / `K58.9`):** The triad of postprandial abdominal pain, abdominal bloating/distension, and unpredictable bowel habits in a 38-year-old female strongly points to Irritable Bowel Syndrome. The descriptor "unpredictable" indicates alternating or fluctuating bowel habits (constipation/diarrhea), aligning most specifically with IBS-M (`K58.2`).
   * **Strict Coding Guideline Alternative (`R19.4` / `R10.9`):** Under strict ICD-10-CM outpatient coding rules, if diagnostic workup is incomplete and an organic cause has not yet been ruled out, symptom codes must be assigned instead of an unconfirmed definitive diagnosis:
     * **`R19.4`** – *Change in bowel habit* (Primary symptom)
     * **`R10.9`** – *Abdominal pain, unspecified*
     * **`R14.0`** – *Abdominal distension (gaseous / bloating)*

2. **Duration and Rome IV Criteria:**
   * Formal Rome IV criteria for IBS require recurrent abdominal pain on average at least 1 day per week in the last 3 months, with symptom onset at least 6 months before diagnosis.
   * Because symptoms have been present for only *"a few weeks,"* this presentation is subacute. Organic causes (e.g., Celiac disease, Inflammatory Bowel Disease, SIBO, or infectious enteritis) require exclusion before finalizing the diagnosis.
