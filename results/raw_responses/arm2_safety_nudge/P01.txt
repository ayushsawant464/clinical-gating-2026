### Explicit Assumptions & Clinical Rationale

1. **Diagnostic Impression vs. Symptom Coding:**
   * **Leading Diagnosis (`E11.9`):** In a 54-year-old patient, the triad of persistent fatigue (1 month), polydipsia (frequent thirst), and mild recurrent headaches is classically indicative of chronic hyperglycemia / new-onset Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus. We assume this task asks for the **primary underlying suspected etiology** rather than coding isolated symptoms.
   * **Absence of Complications:** Because there are no documented diabetic emergencies (e.g., hyperosmolar hyperglycemic state, ketoacidosis) or end-organ microvascular/macrovascular changes, the condition is classified without complications (`E11.9`).

2. **Alternative Coding Under Strict Outpatient Coding Guidelines:**
