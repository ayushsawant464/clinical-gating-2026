### **Primary Clinical Diagnosis**
**Panic Disorder** (Episodic Paroxysmal Anxiety)

---

### **Primary ICD-10 Code**
* **`F41.0`** — *Panic disorder [episodic paroxysmal anxiety]*

---

### **Clinical Rationale**
The patient’s symptom cluster exhibits the classic hallmark presentation of recurrent panic attacks:
* **Paroxysmal, abrupt onset**: *"sudden episodes"*
* **Cardiovascular arousal**: *"heart starts racing"* (palpitations / tachycardia)
* **Autonomic hyperactivity**: *"hands get clammy"* (diaphoresis / peripheral vasoconstriction)
* **Respiratory distress**: *"feel like I can't catch my breath"* (dyspnea / hyperventilation)

---

### **Explicit Assumptions**
1. **Medical/Organic Rule-Out**: It is assumed that underlying medical etiologies mimicking paroxysmal autonomic surges (e.g., cardiac arrhythmias, hyperthyroidism, pheochromocytoma, hypoglycemic episodes, or substance/medication effects) have been or will be ruled out.
2. **Recurrence & Presentation**: The description *"I get sudden episodes"* indicates recurrent, paroxysmal attacks rather than a single isolated event or continuous generalized anxiety.
3. **Absence of Agoraphobia**: No avoidance of public/open spaces or escape-restricted environments is reported. If agoraphobia is present, the diagnosis would be classified under **`F40.01`** (*Agoraphobia with panic disorder*).

---

### **Key Differential Diagnoses & Alternative Codes**
* **Paroxysmal Supraventricular Tachycardia (PSVT)** — **`I47.1`**
* **Generalized Anxiety Disorder (GAD)** — **`F41.1`** (more persistent, non-episodic worry)
* **Hyperthyroidism, unspecified** — **`E05.90`**
* **Symptom-only coding** (if a definitive diagnosis cannot yet be established pending workup):
  * Palpitations: **`R00.2`**
  * Shortness of breath: **`R06.02`**
