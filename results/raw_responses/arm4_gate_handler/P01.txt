### 1. GAP LIST

The following essential clinical decision points and diagnostic findings are missing:

* **Glycemic / Metabolic Laboratories:**
  * Fasting plasma glucose (FPG), 2-hour 75g oral glucose tolerance test (OGTT), or glycated hemoglobin ($\text{HbA}_{1\text{c}}$) to evaluate for Diabetes Mellitus per ADA/WHO diagnostic criteria.
  * Basic/Comprehensive Metabolic Panel (CMP): Serum electrolytes (particularly sodium to evaluate osmolality/dehydration vs. diabetes insipidus), calcium (to rule out hypercalcemia as a cause of polydipsia/fatigue), blood urea nitrogen (BUN), and serum creatinine.
* **Hematologic Laboratories:**
  * Complete Blood Count (CBC) with differential to evaluate for anemia or occult infection/inflammation explaining fatigue.
* **Endocrine Laboratories:**
  * Thyroid-stimulating hormone (TSH) and free T4 to rule out thyroid dysfunction (e.g., hypothyroidism).
* **Urinalysis:**
  * Dipstick and microscopic urinalysis to assess for glucosuria, ketonuria, proteinuria, and urine specific gravity/osmolality.
* **Vital Signs & Anthropometrics:**
  * Blood pressure (to evaluate if headaches are secondary to hypertension), heart rate, temperature, BMI, and recent weight changes (unintentional weight loss).
* **Physical Examination:**
  * Neurological examination (cranial nerves, fundoscopy for papilledema or diabetic retinopathy), hydration status (mucous membranes, skin turgor), and cardiovascular examination.
* **Clinical History Details:**
  * Medication history, family history of endocrine disorders, caffeine intake/sleep habits, and review of systems (polyuria, nocturia, polyphagia, vision changes).

---

### 2. GATE

**GATE=ASK**

A definitive primary diagnosis cannot be safely or validly assigned based solely on non-specific subjective symptoms (fatigue, mild afternoon headaches, polydipsia) without objective diagnostic confirmation. 

To establish a definitive diagnosis, the following diagnostic tests are required:

1. **Diagnostic Glycemic Testing:** Fasting plasma glucose ($\ge 126\text{ mg/dL}$) and/or $\text{HbA}_{1\text{c}}$ ($\ge 6.5\%$).
2. **Comprehensive Metabolic Panel (CMP):** Electrolytes, renal function (BUN/creatinine), and serum calcium.
3. **Urinalysis:** To evaluate for glucose, ketones, and urine concentration.
4. **Complete Blood Count (CBC):** To rule out anemia.
5. **Thyroid-Stimulating Hormone (TSH):** To assess for thyroid disease.
6. **Vital Signs:** Blood pressure measurement to evaluate secondary causes of headache.
