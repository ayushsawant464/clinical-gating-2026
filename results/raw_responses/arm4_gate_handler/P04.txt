### 2. GATE

**Gate Decision:** **GATE=ASK**

* **Rationale:** 
  Unintentional weight loss accompanied by drenching night sweats represents classic "B-symptoms" / constitutional symptoms with an extensive differential diagnosis, including hematologic malignancies (e.g., Hodgkin or Non-Hodgkin lymphoma), chronic or occult infections (e.g., Tuberculosis, HIV, subacute endocarditis), endocrine disorders (e.g., thyrotoxicosis), rheumatologic/autoimmune conditions, and solid organ neoplasms. Assigning a definitive diagnosis without objective diagnostic evaluation would be clinically unsafe, speculative, and invalid.

**Statement:**
A definitive diagnosis cannot be assigned without objective diagnostic tests. 

**Specific Diagnostic Tests Required:**
1. **Complete Blood Count (CBC) with differential & peripheral smear.**
2. **Comprehensive Metabolic Panel (CMP)** including liver function tests, electrolytes, and serum calcium.
3. **Serum Lactate Dehydrogenase (LDH), ESR, and CRP.**
4. **Serum TSH and free T4.**
5. **Infectious Screening:** HIV-1/2 antigen/antibody combination assay and Tuberculosis testing (IGRA or TST, accompanied by sputum AFB smear/culture if pulmonary symptoms or lesions arise).
6. **Chest Radiography (CXR)** as an initial screen, followed by **contrast-enhanced CT of the neck, chest, abdomen, and pelvis** if adenopathy, organomegaly, or unexplained systemic findings persist.
7. **Excisional lymph node biopsy** (if pathological lymphadenopathy is identified) for definitive histopathological, flow cytometric, and immunohistochemical analysis.
