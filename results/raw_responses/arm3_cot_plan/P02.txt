### Classification

* **Primary Clinical Diagnosis:** Angina Pectoris (Exertional / Stable Angina)
* **Primary ICD-10 Code:** **I20.9** (*Angina pectoris, unspecified*)

#### Clinical Rationale:
* **Presentation:** A 62-year-old male experiencing predictable, dull retrosternal discomfort provoked by exertion (walking up stairs) is the classic presentation of exertional myocardial ischemia (angina pectoris). Associated lightheadedness may reflect transiently reduced cardiac output or autonomic responses during exertion.
* **Coding Rationale:** In the absence of documented coronary angiographic findings confirming underlying native coronary artery disease (which would otherwise code to `I25.11-`), exertional/stable angina without further specification codes to **I20.9**.
