### Diagnostic Impression & Clinical Differential

The constellation of unexplained weight loss and drenching night sweats represents **B symptoms / constitutional symptoms**, which warrant immediate clinical investigation for:

1. **Lymphoma / Hematologic Malignancy** 
   * *Hodgkin lymphoma, unspecified*: **`C81.90`**
   * *Non-Hodgkin lymphoma, unspecified*: **`C85.90`**
2. **Chronic Systemic Infection**
   * *Respiratory tuberculosis*: **`A15.0`** / **`A16.9`**
   * *Human immunodeficiency virus (HIV) disease*: **`B20`**
3. **Endocrine / Metabolic Disorder**
   * *Thyrotoxicosis / Hyperthyroidism, unspecified*: **`E05.90`**

*Note: If an underlying etiology is subsequently confirmed via laboratory, imaging, or histopathological evaluation, that definitive diagnosis would supersede `R63.4` as the primary ICD-10 code.*
