### Clinical Rationale & Details

* **Clinical Presentation:** 
  * Misplacing common objects and progressive short-term forgetfulness combined with **temporal disorientation** (confusion about the day of the week) exceed typical age-associated memory decline.
  * In the absence of documented functional loss or inability to perform basic and instrumental activities of daily living (ADLs/IADLs), the clinical presentation meets the core criteria for **amnestic Mild Cognitive Impairment (aMCI)**, which represents a transitional state between normal aging and early dementia.

* **Alternative / Symptom-Level Coding:**
  * **`R41.3`** (*Other amnesia / Memory loss, unspecified*) or **`R41.89`** (*Other symptoms and signs involving cognitive functions and awareness*): Applicable if billing strictly at the chief complaint/symptom level prior to objective neurocognitive confirmation.

* **Primary Differentials to Rule Out:**
  * **Alzheimer's Disease / Major Neurocognitive Disorder (ICD-10: `G30.9` / `F03.90`):** Should functional assessment reveal impairment in managing finances, medications, or other IADLs.
  * **Depression / Pseudodementia (ICD-10: `F32.9` / `F32.A`):** Can present with subjective memory complaints and concentration deficits in older adults.
  * **Reversible Metabolic/Systemic Causes:** Vitamin B12 deficiency (`E53.8`), hypothyroidism (`E03.9`), normal pressure hydrocephalus (`G91.2`), or polypharmacy/sedative effects.

* **Recommended Diagnostic Evaluation:**
  * **Cognitive Screening:** Montreal Cognitive Assessment (MoCA) or Mini-Mental State Examination (MMSE).
  * **Functional Assessment:** Functional Activities Questionnaire (FAQ) to verify ADL independence.
  * **Laboratory Panel:** Serum B12, TSH, comprehensive metabolic panel (CMP), and complete blood count (CBC).
  * **Structural Neuroimaging:** Brain MRI (without contrast) to assess for hippocampal volume loss, vascular burden, or other structural pathology.
