### **Primary Clinical Diagnosis**
**Obstructive Sleep Apnea (OSA)** *(also referred to as Obstructive Sleep Apnea Syndrome - OSAS)*

---

### **Primary ICD-10 Code**
* **ICD-10-CM:** **`G47.33`** — *Obstructive sleep apnea (adult) (pediatric)*

*(Alternative broad code if unconfirmed by polysomnography: `G47.30` – Sleep apnea, unspecified)*

---

### **Clinical Rationale**
* **Witnessed Apneas:** Pauses in breathing observed by a sleep partner are a cardinal indicator of sleep-disordered breathing.
* **Nocturnal Symptoms:** Loud, chronic snoring indicates upper airway narrowing/vibratory flow limitation during sleep.
* **Daytime Consequences:** Trouble concentrating at work and physical signs of chronic sleep disruption (periorbital hyperpigmentation/dark circles) reflect fragmented sleep architecture and nocturnal hypoxia.
* **Demographics:** Middle-aged male presentation fits classic epidemiological risk factors.

---

### **Recommended Diagnostic Workup**
* **Objective Testing:** Diagnostic overnight polysomnography (PSG) or Home Sleep Apnea Testing (HSAT) to calculate the Apnea-Hypopnea Index (AHI).
* **Screening Assessment:** Administration of standardized screening tools such as the Epworth Sleepiness Scale (ESS) and STOP-Bang questionnaire.
