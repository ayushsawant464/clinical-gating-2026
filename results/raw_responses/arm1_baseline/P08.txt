### Primary Clinical Diagnosis
* **Diagnosis:** **Panic Disorder** (Panic Attack / Episodic Paroxysmal Anxiety)
* **Primary ICD-10 Code:** **`F41.0`** (*Panic disorder [without agoraphobia]*)

---

### Clinical Rationale
The patient presents with classic hallmark symptoms of an acute **panic attack** (episodic paroxysmal anxiety):
* **Paroxysmal onset:** Discrete, sudden episodes.
* **Autonomic hyperactivity:** Palpitations/tachycardia ("heart starts racing") and diaphoresis ("hands get clammy").
* **Respiratory symptoms:** Dyspnea / hyperventilation sensation ("can't catch my breath").

---

### Key Differential Diagnoses (to rule out clinically)
1. **Paroxysmal Supraventricular Tachycardia (PSVT)** – ICD-10: `I47.1`
2. **Hyperthyroidism / Thyrotoxicosis** – ICD-10: `E05.90`
3. **Pheochromocytoma** – ICD-10: `D35.00`
4. **Generalized Anxiety Disorder (GAD)** – ICD-10: `F41.1` *(characterized by persistent, non-episodic worry)*
