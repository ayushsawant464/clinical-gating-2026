### **Primary Clinical Diagnosis**
**Mild Cognitive Impairment (MCI) — Amnestic Type**  
*(Differential / Early Working Diagnosis: Early-Stage Alzheimer's Disease / Unspecified Dementia)*

---

### **Primary ICD-10-CM Code**
* **`G31.84`** — *Mild cognitive impairment, so stated*

---

### **Clinical Rationale & Coding Breakdown**

1. **Symptom Presentation**:
   * **Episodic memory loss**: Becoming increasingly forgetful and misplacing everyday items (keys).
   * **Temporal disorientation**: Confusion regarding the day of the week in a 71-year-old patient.
   * At this initial stage, cognitive decline is noted beyond normal age-related changes, but without documented evidence of severe disruption to independent basic activities of daily living (ADLs), this clinically fits **amnestic MCI** (frequently prodromal Alzheimer’s disease).

2. **Differential / Context-Dependent Coding**:
   * **If formal dementia / Alzheimer's disease is confirmed following full evaluation (e.g., MoCA/MMSE, neuroimaging, lab work):**
     * **`G30.9`** — *Alzheimer's disease, unspecified* (or **`G30.1`** for *Alzheimer's disease with late onset*), with secondary code **`F02.80`** (*Dementia in other diseases classified elsewhere, unspecified severity, without behavioral disturbance*).
     * **`F03.90`** — *Unspecified dementia, unspecified severity, without behavioral disturbance*.
   * **If coded strictly at the sign/symptom level (provisional/pre-diagnostic workup per ICD-10 guidelines):**
     * **`R41.3`** — *Other amnesia (memory loss, unspecified)*
     * **`R41.0`** — *Disorientation, unspecified*
