### **Primary Clinical Diagnosis**
* **Diagnosis:** **Stable Angina Pectoris** (Exertional Angina / Angina of Effort)

---

### **Primary ICD-10 Code**
* **ICD-10-CM Code:** **`I20.9`** — *Angina pectoris, unspecified*
  *(Alternative specific code under ICD-10-CM: **`I20.89`** / WHO ICD-10: **`I20.8`** — Other forms of angina pectoris / Angina of effort)*

---

### **Clinical Rationale**
1. **Symptom Pattern:** The patient presents with classic features of exertional chest discomfort—a dull ache provoked by increased myocardial oxygen demand (walking up stairs).
2. **Associated Symptoms:** Episodic lightheadedness (presyncope) during or following exertion can occur secondary to transient hypoperfusion, dysrhythmia, or reduced stroke volume during ischemic episodes.
3. **Key Differential Diagnoses to Investigate:**
   * **Coronary Artery Disease (CAD):** The underlying atherosclerotic etiology for stable angina (`I25.10` / `I25.119`).
   * **Aortic Valve Stenosis:** The combination of exertional angina and presyncope/lightheadedness in an older adult male warrants urgent cardiac auscultation and echocardiography to rule out hemodynamically significant aortic stenosis (`I35.0`).
   * **Unstable Angina:** If the onset is recent (<2 months), escalating in frequency, or occurs at rest (`I20.0`).
