Depending on documentation and encounter type (e.g., formal outpatient coding guidelines prior to serologic/radiographic confirmation):

1. **Suspected Etiologic Diagnosis (Working Diagnosis):**
   * **Diagnosis:** **Rheumatoid Arthritis, unspecified**
   * **ICD-10 Code:** **`M06.9`**
   * *Rationale:* In a 29-year-old female, new-onset episodic polyarticular joint pain involving the hands (small joints) and knees (large joints) coupled with morning fatigue/stiffness is the classic hallmark presentation of early inflammatory arthritis, predominantly **Rheumatoid Arthritis** (or alternatively, early **Systemic Lupus Erythematosus**, `M32.9`).

### **Recommended Workup to Confirm Diagnosis**
* **Serology:** Rheumatoid Factor (RF), anti-cyclic citrullinated peptide (anti-CCP), Antinuclear Antibodies (ANA).
* **Inflammatory Markers:** Erythrocyte Sedimentation Rate (ESR), C-Reactive Protein (CRP).
* **Imaging & Physical Exam:** Bilateral hand and knee radiographs/ultrasound, evaluation for joint tenderness, swelling (synovitis), and duration of morning stiffness.
