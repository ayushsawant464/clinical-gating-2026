### **Primary Clinical Diagnosis**
**Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus (T2DM)** *(Suspected / Presumptive)*

* **Primary ICD-10 Code:** **`E11.9`** (*Type 2 diabetes mellitus without complications*)  
  *(Category: **`E11`** – Type 2 diabetes mellitus)*

---

### **Clinical Rationale**
The patient’s symptom triad is a classic initial presentation for new-onset Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus in a middle-aged adult:
1. **Polydipsia (Excessive Thirst):** High circulating blood glucose exceeds the renal threshold, resulting in osmotic diuresis (polyuria) and compensatory, persistent thirst.
2. **Persistent Fatigue (1 month duration):** Impaired cellular glucose uptake due to insulin resistance diminishes cellular energy production.
3. **Afternoon Headaches:** Commonly caused by postprandial glycemic fluctuations (hyperglycemia or reactive hypoglycemia) and mild osmotic dehydration over the course of the day.

---

Under standard outpatient **ICD-10-CM coding guidelines** (Section IV.H), if diagnostic laboratory testing has not yet been documented to formally confirm diabetes, the primary presenting signs/symptoms may be coded until confirmation is established:

### **Recommended Next Steps & Confirmatory Workup**
* **Laboratory Testing:**
  * Fasting Plasma Glucose (FPG) and/or 2-hour Oral Glucose Tolerance Test (OGTT)
  * Glycated Hemoglobin ($\text{HbA}_{1c}$)
  * Urinalysis (evaluating for glucosuria, proteinuria, or ketonuria)
  * Basic Metabolic Panel (BMP) to assess renal function and electrolytes
* **Key Differentials to Exclude:** Impaired fasting glucose / pre-diabetes (`R73.03`), primary hypothyroidism (`E03.9`), chronic dehydration (`E86.0`), and diabetes insipidus (`E23.2`).
